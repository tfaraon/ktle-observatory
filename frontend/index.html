Kati Thanda–
Lake Eyre

Australia’s lowest point and largest salt lake, dry for most of its history and, when the rivers of its basin reach it, an inland sea for a while. This observatory follows its water from space, alongside its natural history, its catchment and the Aboriginal peoples whose country it is.
